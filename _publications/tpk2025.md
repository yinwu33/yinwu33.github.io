Interaction and kinematic priors for all agent classes (vehicles, pedestrians and cyclists) are integrated into a transformer-based trajectory predictor. A rule-based interaction importance score (DG-SFM) makes agent interactions interpretable, and class-specific kinematic models, including a new pedestrian model, eliminate physically infeasible predictions on Argoverse 2.
