Reactive parking requires reaching an assigned slot with sub-meter precision while responding to nearby vehicles. CoPark trains a single policy through multi-agent self-play, combining a precomputed offline plan with a learned reactive residual, and shows emergent behaviors such as reverse-yielding, mid-maneuver yielding, tight-corridor passing and queuing.
