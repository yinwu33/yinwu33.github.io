Trajectory prediction is formulated as a constrained regression guided by permissible driving directions and their left and right boundaries. The network predicts acceleration profiles along these paths, which keeps predictions on the road and kinematically feasible. On Argoverse 2, the approach eliminates infeasible trajectories and reduces the off-road rate under adversarial attacks from 66% to 1%.
